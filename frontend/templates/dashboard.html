About CardioAI

CardioAI leverages an explainable AI risk-scoring engine to analyze patient health data for early detection of cardiovascular disease risk. The system evaluates 18 clinical parameters including lipid profiles, blood pressure readings, lifestyle factors, and medical history — providing transparent, actionable insights aligned with clinical cardiology guidelines.
